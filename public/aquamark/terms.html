What the bot does

Protected posts and leak tracing (AquaMark Pro)

  • Servers using protected posts must tell their members that copies are marked to the member who viewed them and that staff can see who viewed each post. The bot also shows “This copy is marked to you” on every copy.
  • Trace results are estimates with a confidence score, not proof. Heavily edited, cropped, rotated or re-photographed images may not be traceable, or may be matched with low confidence.
  • Server staff alone decide whether to act on a trace result. The bot never removes roles, assigns roles or bans anyone unless a staff member presses a button. We are not responsible for actions a server takes against its members.
  • Watermarks deter and help identify leaks; they can't prevent screenshots or guarantee your content won't be shared.
