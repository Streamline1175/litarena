Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

This policy explains what the AquaMark Discord bot (“the bot”, “we”) collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your Discord user ID, with your saved presets and default settingsTo reuse your watermark settings
Logos you save, plus the last logo you attachedSo saved logos and “Same as last time” work
Watermark history: settings used, links to results, watermark IDs and image fingerprints (never the image itself). You can turn this off with /history recordSo /history and /verify can recognise your work
Usage counts and feedback you send, with your user ID and usernameTo fix problems and improve the bot
Server ID and name, and per-server settingsTo apply the server's settings and plan
Pro servers: the original images of each protected post, and its captionTo create each member's marked copy when they press View
Pro servers: view records: which member viewed which protected post, when, the code and pattern issued to them, and fingerprints of their copySo staff can see who viewed a post and trace a leaked copy
Pro servers: brand kits (logo, text, colours), auto-watermark channels, mod-log and role settings, and trace resultsTo run Pro features
Subscription records: plan, dates, and for card payments the Stripe customer and subscription IDsTo give paying servers their plan

Images you watermark with the free tools are processed and sent back to you; we don't keep them. Background removal runs on our own server, not a third-party service. In auto-watermark channels on Pro servers, the bot reads images members post so it can watermark them; it does not store message text. We never see or store card numbers.

Marked copies (AquaMark Pro)

When you view a protected post, your copy carries a faint visible code, a hidden pattern and an invisible signed mark that identify you to that server's staff. Staff can see that you viewed the post and, if a copy is shared outside the server, may use it to identify you. Your copy itself is cached only briefly so it can be resent quickly.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Data reaches these services only as far as needed to run the bot:

  • Discord, to deliver messages and check subscriptions (Discord's privacy policy).
  • Stripe, for card payments (Stripe's privacy policy).
  • Server staff on Pro servers, who see view records and trace results for their own server's protected posts.
  • Bot listing sites such as top.gg, which receive only the bot's server count and update announcements.

How long we keep it

  • Presets, logos, history and usage counts: until you delete them or run /mydata delete.
  • Protected post originals: until staff remove the post (/protect remove) or the bot leaves the server.
  • View records: while the server keeps the bot, so a leak can still be traced later. They are deleted when the bot leaves the server. /mydata delete does not remove them, so a member can't erase evidence of a leak; ask the server's staff or contact us if you believe a record should be removed.
  • Cached member copies: briefly, then discarded.
  • Subscription and trial records: kept while needed to apply plans and enforce trial limits.
  • Logs, which include user and server IDs and the commands used: kept for troubleshooting and cleared during maintenance.

Your choices

  • /mydata export downloads everything linked to your user ID; /mydata delete removes it, except Pro view records as explained above.
  • Turn off watermark history with /history record enabled:False.
  • For any other request, ask in the support server. We handle requests within 30 days.

Children

The bot is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age (13, or higher where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Security

Data is stored on a private server. Invisible marks are signed with a private key so they can't be forged. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and announce significant changes through the bot's update announcements.
